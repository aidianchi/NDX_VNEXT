## 一、报告的形制（从 Issue 145/146 的目录页看）

Top of Mind 每一期是一个主题，由"编辑 Allison Nathan 访谈多位专家/分析师 + 分析师短评小节"组装而成。目录页直接列出每个受访者的名字、头衔，以及**每人一句黑体核心观点**：

> INTERVIEWS WITH:
> Hal Brands, Professor, Johns Hopkins SAIS…
> **"President Trump has long believed that the US can't be the preeminent global power if it's not the preeminent regional power." — Hal Brands**
> …
> **"The [US-Europe] relationship looks to be headed for a rocky period, but not a divorce." — Ivan Krastev**

（Issue 145《Americas First》目录页，索引摘录）

语言观察：读者翻开目录页就能拿走全部核心观点，不用读任何一段正文。这是"结论先行"在最表层的落地——**观点直接印在目录里**。

## 二、正文 Q&A 体例样本（Issue 142《Data (Un)Reliability》，索引摘录）

正文是编辑与受访者的问答。每个受访者出场时，先有一段编辑写的"身份+他要论证什么"的引子：

> Erica Groshen is former Commissioner of the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) (2013-17). … Below, she outlines the BLS' mounting challenges and what they could mean for data reliability ahead. The views stated herein are those of the interviewee and do not necessarily reflect those of Goldman Sachs.
>
> **Allison Nathan: You previously served as BLS Commissioner. Walk us through how the BLS collects data for the key economic indicators it reports and how that process has evolved.**
>
> Erica Groshen: The BLS tailors each program according to the measurement objectives and sample population, but the general approach centers on modern survey methodology that defines the survey universe, designs an appropriate—often stratified—sample, and recruits survey participants. Survey responses are then run through models that output final numbers indicative of the broader universe. …

语言观察：问句本身是完整句子，直接点明要对方回答什么；答句第一段先给总括（"general approach centers on…"），再展开细节。每个问答都是一个小金字塔。

## 三、访谈中的完整推理链样本（播客文字稿，全文抓取，2026-09-09 播出）

受访者 Donald Kohn（前美联储副主席）的一段话，展示"观点→理由→实例→折中立场"的完整链条：

> Donald Kohn: I think more serious is the forward guidance issue. So, here I have a little sympathy with Chairman Warsh. His concern is the forward guidance. What are we going to do next is both constraining the committee and constraining the market reaction to incoming information. And I think a good example where it constrained the committee with adverse consequences was coming out of COVID. So, the forward guidance was we're going to hold rates at zero until we're at full employment almost no matter what the inflation rate is. … So, I think that forward guidance was not well-conceived. And I think it did constrain the committee, which is not to say that some guidance isn't helpful and at the zero lower bound may be necessary.

语言观察：句子里嵌着"程度限定"（"a little sympathy"、"not to say that some guidance isn't helpful"）——观点不靠斩钉截铁取胜，靠把边界说清楚取胜。
