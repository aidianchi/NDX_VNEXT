# 样本 04：Howard Marks 备忘录《Nobody Knows (Yet Again)》

- **来源 URL**：`https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/nobody-knows-yet-again`（全文抓取成功）
- **抓取日期**：2026-09-18
- **作者与出处**：Howard Marks，Oaktree Capital Management 联席董事长，2025-04-09 发布（特朗普关税冲击后的市场动荡期）。Marks 的备忘录被巴菲特公开推荐（"我一看到邮件里有 Howard Marks 的备忘录，我总是第一时间打开读"——此语流传甚广，本次未核到一手出处，标注为待验证）。
- **全文状态**：全文。

## 一、开头：用一段叙事交代"我为什么写这篇、我的立场是什么"

> On Monday, September 15, 2008, shortly after the close of the New York Stock Exchange, Lehman Brothers surprised the world by filing for bankruptcy. … Market participants quickly concluded that the U.S. financial sector was on the verge of melting down.
>
> I thought I should comment on these developments and the outlook, and the result was a memo called Nobody Knows, published four days later. I affirmed my ignorance of the future as usual, but to an even greater degree given that all prior expectations had been upended. Nobody knew – especially me – whether the spiral could be arrested. Nevertheless, I concluded that we had to assume it would, and thus that we should plow money into financial assets at their highly discounted prices.

语言观察：开头不喊结论，先交代时间、事件、自己当时写了什么、当时的推理是什么。结论（"应该往里投钱"）嵌在叙事里给出，附带明确的自认无知（"Nobody knew – especially me"）。

## 二、用 bullet 把推理的骨架亮出来

> There was nothing anyone could say they "knew," and that included me. I was limited to gaming out my conclusions, which were as follows:
>
> - we can't confidently predict the end of the world,
> - we'd have no idea what to do if we knew the world would end,
> - the things we'd do to gird for the end of the world would be disastrous if it didn't end, and
> - most of the time the world doesn't end.

语言观察：四条前提全是短句、全有主语，层层咬合推导出行动（投钱）。bullet 在这里不是装饰，是**把推理链的每一环拆开给读者检查**。

## 三、坦诚不确定性是有内容量的写法

> **I want to say right here about 2008 and the other crises I've invested through – as well as today – that I don't reach my conclusions with confidence or act without trepidation.** There's absolutely no place for certainty in the world of investing, and that's particularly true at turning points and during upheavals. I'm never sure my answers are right, but if I can reason out what's most logical, I feel I have to move in that direction.

> In addition to a forecast, you also need a good sense for the probability your forecast is correct, since not all forecasts are created equal.

语言观察：他反复说"我不确定"，但每次说"不确定"都跟着一句"那怎么办"——不确定不是终点，是推理的起点。关键句用黑体标出，让快读的人能抓住骨架。

## 四、长推理链的组织：关税一章

备忘录中段讨论关税，组织方式是：先列出关税的全部目标（八条 bullet），给一句公允评价（"It must be acknowledged that every one of these things is desirable in itself"）；然后转折（"If only it were that easy"），列出二阶三阶后果（六条 bullet）；然后逐条展开——每条展开都是"机制→例子→数字"的顺序，例如：

> There is little doubt that the tariffs will raise prices. Tariffs are taxes on imports, and someone has to pay them. … While it's the importer who pays the tariff at the border, the cost is usually passed on to the ultimate purchaser of the goods, the consumer.

> …as I wrote in the memo Economic Reality in May 2016: How will the interests of the 3.2 million Americans estimated to have lost their manufacturing jobs to China be balanced against the hundreds of millions who would have to pay considerably more for imported goods? Not an easy question.

语言观察：机制解释用日常因果（"关税是对进口征的税，总得有人付"），数字嵌在问句里（3.2 million 对 hundreds of millions），还不时回引自己九年前的旧文——**推理链横跨多年保持连贯，自己跟自己对话**。

## 五、结尾：The Bottom Line

> **I consider the tariff developments thus far to be what soccer fans call an "own goal"** …
>
> Everyone was happy to buy 18-24-36 months ago, when the horizon was cloudless and asset prices were sky-high. Now, with heretofore unimaginable risks on the table and priced in, it's appropriate to sniff around for bargains: the babies that are being thrown out with the bath water. We're on the case.

语言观察：备忘录体是"结论殿后"的体裁——行动结论（"我们在找便宜货了"）放在最后一段。这与金字塔式相反，是有意识的体裁选择：它服务的是信任建立（陪你走完推理全程），不是快读。代价是：10 分钟读不完，必须从头读到尾。
