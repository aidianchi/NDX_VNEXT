## 一、写作方法的自述（SEC《A Plain English Handbook》序言，1998）

来源：`https://www.sec.gov/pdf/handbook.pdf`（经 Stradley 律所汇编版转录核对，抓取日期 2026-09-18）

> One unoriginal but useful tip: Write with a specific person in mind. When writing Berkshire Hathaway's annual report, I pretend that I'm talking to my sisters. I have no trouble picturing them: though highly intelligent, they are not experts in accounting or finance.

> Too often, I've been unable to decipher just what is being said or, worse yet, had to conclude that nothing was being said. … Perhaps the most common problem, however, is that a well-intentioned and informed writer simply fails to get the message across to an intelligent, interested reader. In that case, stilted jargon and complex constructions are usually the villains.

语言观察：巴菲特把"看懂"的责任划给作者——读者看不懂，首先怀疑写的人自己没想清楚或者语言装腔作势，而不是读者水平不够。假想一个具体读者（姐姐）是他的核心方法。

## 二、信的抬头：先交代"这封信的契约"

> "Report," however, implies a greater responsibility. In addition to the mandated data, we believe we owe you additional commentary about what you own and how we think. Our goal is to communicate with you in a manner that we would wish you to use if our positions were reversed – that is, if you were Berkshire's CEO while I and my family were passive investors, trusting you with our savings.

语言观察：开头第一段不谈业绩，先谈"我凭什么给你写、我怎么写"——立场互换测试（如果我坐你的位置，我希望收到什么样的信）。

## 三、谈错误：数字当证据，不当装饰

> During the 2019-23 period, I have used the words "mistake" or "error" 16 times in my letters to you. Many other huge companies have never used either word over that span. … I have also been a director of large public companies at which "mistake" or "wrong" were forbidden words at board meetings or analyst calls.

语言观察：连"我坦诚"这件事都用可核查的数字来证明（16 次），而不是自我标榜。

## 四、叙事+数字嵌句：Pete Liegl 收购案

> I first heard of Forest River – the Indiana company Pete founded and managed – on June 21, 2005. On that day I received a letter from an intermediary detailing relevant data about the company, a recreational vehicle ("RV") manufacturer. … "Well, I looked at Berkshire's proxy statement and I wouldn't want to make more than my boss, so pay me $100,000 per year." … During the next 19 years, Pete shot the lights out. No competitor came close to his performance.

语言观察：讲故事时日期、价格、薪酬数字都在句子里；术语第一次出现就带解释（recreational vehicle ("RV")——先给全名再给缩写，之后直接用 RV）。

## 五、长跨度叙事里的数字锚点：60 年的税务故事

> Sixty years ago, present management took control of Berkshire. That move was a mistake – my mistake – and one that plagued us for two decades. … In 1965, the company did not pay a dime of income tax … Fast forward 60 years and imagine the surprise at the Treasury when that same company – still operating under the name of Berkshire Hathaway – paid far more in corporate income tax than the U.S. government had ever received from any company…

语言观察：跨越 60 年的故事用两个年份数字锚住两头（1965 年一分税没交；2024 年交了史上最多的企业所得税），中间不堆细节。句子短，动词具体（"did not pay a dime"）。

## 六、句长与节奏（全文观感）

全信段落多在 3-6 句之间，大量段落只有一两句；破折号、口语插入语（"I picked myself off the floor"）频繁出现，但每句的主语-谓语结构完整。标题是短语加结论（"Mistakes – Yes, We Make Them at Berkshire"、"Surprise, Surprise! An Important American Record is Smashed"）——**小标题本身就是一句可读的结论**。
